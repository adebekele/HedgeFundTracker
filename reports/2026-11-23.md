## Notes

- 13Fs are filed within 45 days of quarter-end and only cover long US equities/options -- no shorts, macro, FX, futures, or cash. For macro-oriented funds (Druckenmiller, Soros, Bridgewater) this captures a small slice of the actual strategy.
- Because most funds only refile quarterly, most weekly checks will show "no change" in the underlying data -- this run mainly catches newly posted or amended filings within days rather than waiting for the next manual check.
